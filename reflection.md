# Mission Reflection

## Which cloud platform impressed you the most?
Google Cloud Platform impressed me most. Its clean console, performance, and leadership in Kubernetes and AI show a modern, forward-thinking design. It feels built for how applications are developed today—containerized and data-driven.

## What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
All three offer the same core building blocks: virtual machines, object storage, virtual networking, managed databases, and identity systems. They all follow pay-as-you-go pricing, offer Availability Zones for reliability, and provide auto-scaling. The concepts are nearly identical—only names and interfaces differ.

## How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?
The right cloud is always the one that matches the client’s specific needs. A Microsoft shop chooses Azure for integration; an AI company chooses GCP for tools; a startup may choose AWS for Free Tier and resources. You don’t pick the most popular—you pick the best fit.

## What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?
I learned that cloud decisions are business decisions first. Technical features matter, but existing systems, budget, team skills, and future growth matter just as much. Asking "what problem are we solving?" is always the first step.

## How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved?
My portfolio now shows research, comparison, and recommendations—moving from "what is cloud" to "how do I choose cloud." The organized structure, screenshots, and Markdown reports make it something I can actually show to employers.
